# The Stapler Study

In 2026, a team of three interns set out to measure the world's slowest office stapler. Dr. Patel supervised.

The first staple took 41 minutes. The second took 41 minutes and one second, which the team called a regression.

Marcus: Are we sure it's plugged in?

Dana: It's a stapler, Marcus.

[pause 1.2s]

---

After six weeks, the study concluded that the stapler was, in fact, a hole punch.
